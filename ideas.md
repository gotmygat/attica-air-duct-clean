## CHOSEN: Direction B — "Clean Air Luxury"

Rationale: Allora Day Spa's elegance translates perfectly to a premium home services brand. The warm neutrals and editorial typography signal trust and quality. The vivid green accent connects to "clean air" and Attica's existing brand green. This approach is daring in its restraint — most home services sites are cluttered and loud; this will feel like a breath of fresh air.
